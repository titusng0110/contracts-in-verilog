# 12. Notices

&emsp;(a) **_Effectiveness._** Any notice or other communication in respect of this Agreement may be given in any manner described below (except that a notice or other communication under Section 5 or 6 may not be given by electronic messaging system or e-mail) to the address or number or in accordance with the electronic messaging system or e-mail details provided (see the Schedule) and will be deemed effective as indicated: ―

&emsp;&emsp;(i) if in writing and delivered in person or by courier, on the date it is delivered;

&emsp;&emsp;(ii) if sent by telex, on the date the recipient’s answerback is received;

&emsp;&emsp;(iii) if sent by facsimile transmission, on the date it is received by a responsible employee of the recipient in legible form (it being agreed that the burden of proving receipt will be on the sender and will not be met by a transmission report generated by the sender’s facsimile machine);

&emsp;&emsp;(iv) if sent by certified or registered mail (airmail, if overseas) or the equivalent (return receipt requested), on the date it is delivered or its delivery is attempted;

&emsp;&emsp;(v) if sent by electronic messaging system, on the date it is received; or

&emsp;&emsp;(vi) if sent by e-mail, on the date it is delivered,

&emsp;unless the date of that delivery (or attempted delivery) or that receipt, as applicable, is not a Local Business Day or that communication is delivered (or attempted) or received, as applicable, after the close of business on a Local Business Day, in which case that communication will be deemed given and effective on the first following day that is a Local Business Day.

&emsp;(b) **_Change of Details._** Either party may by notice to the other change the address, telex or facsimile number or electronic messaging system or e-mail details at which notices or other communications are to be given to it.
